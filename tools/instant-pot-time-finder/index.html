Before you cook

These times are estimates for electric pressure cooking, not guarantees of doneness. Food thickness, quantity, altitude and appliance model affect results. Use your model's required minimum thin liquid; never exceed its fill limit. For beans, grains, soups and other expanding or foaming foods, follow the half-full limit and allow natural release. Never force the lid open. Keep clear of steam and follow your model's venting instructions.

Use a thermometer for meat, poultry and fish. See safe internal temperatures and Instant Pot safety and operating guidance. Dry kidney and lima beans are not included pending additional toxin-specific preparation review. Release recommendations are conservative adaptations, not necessarily identical to the cited time tables.
