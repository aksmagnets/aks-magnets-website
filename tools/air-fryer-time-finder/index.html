Before you cook

These are starting estimates, not guarantees of doneness. Cooking times can change with food thickness, basket loading, and appliance model. Preheating is not included. Check food partway through and shake or flip where appropriate.

For meat, poultry and seafood, use a food thermometer. For example, chicken should reach 165°F (74°C) internally. Consult FoodSafety.gov's safe temperature guide. Follow package directions for prepared frozen foods. Some entries are based on particular air fryer models; never use a temperature above your appliance’s rated maximum.
