Tips for using the chart

Start with the portion size

The recommended time depends on the amount of food and its thickness. Match the size or quantity column as closely as possible.

Check partway through

Shake the basket or turn food halfway through. For smaller portions, start checking early to avoid overcooking.

Check doneness safely

Cooking times alone cannot confirm that meat, poultry or seafood is safe. Use a food thermometer and follow current food-safety guidance.

Allow for your appliance

Air fryer models, basket crowding and starting food temperature can change cooking time. Use these figures as a starting reference.

Frequently asked questions

Are these times for a preheated air fryer?

The chart does not include preheating time. Follow your air fryer's instructions for whether and how long to preheat.

Do I need to flip food in an air fryer?

For best results, shake the basket or flip food about halfway through cooking. Some delicate foods may need gentler handling.

Why does my food take longer than the chart says?

Food thickness, starting temperature, quantity, basket loading and differences between appliances can all affect cooking time.

How can I check if chicken is cooked?

Use a food thermometer in the thickest part of the chicken. As a general U.S. food-safety guideline, chicken should reach 165°F (74°C) internally. See FoodSafety.gov's temperature chart for additional foods.
