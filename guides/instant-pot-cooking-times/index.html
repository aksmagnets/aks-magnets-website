Using this pressure-cooking chart

Use High pressure

The times on the AKS Magnets reference magnet assume the High pressure-cooking setting.

Brown meat first where indicated

For beef, pork and poultry, the magnet recommends first browning or searing meat with the Sauté/Brown setting. This improves flavour and colour.

Use a basket or trivet

For vegetables and other foods marked by the steamer-basket symbol on the magnet, use an appropriate basket or trivet.

Measure liquid carefully

“Covered” means add liquid to cover the food; for pasta and grains, “×” indicates the liquid amount relative to the food amount. Check that your model's minimum-liquid and fill-limit requirements are met.

Natural vs. quick pressure release

N — Natural release

Allow pressure to fall on its own after the cooking countdown finishes. Do not force the lid open.

Q — Quick release

Release pressure using your model's instructions, keeping your hands and face away from steam. Never vent foods in a manner prohibited by the appliance manual.

Frequently asked questions

Do these cooking times include preheating?

No. The listed time is the pressure-cooking countdown only. Time to reach pressure and to release pressure is additional.

What does “+8” in the Frozen column mean?

The magnet recommends adding eight minutes to the listed pressure-cooking time for that frozen food. A value of N/A means the magnet does not provide a frozen adjustment for that entry.

How much liquid do I need for rice or grains?

For grain entries such as “1×” or “2×,” the chart expresses liquid as a ratio relative to the listed food amount. Actual minimum liquid requirements depend on your Instant Pot model and recipe.

Are these times guaranteed for every Instant Pot?

No. Food size, starting temperature, ingredients, altitude and model can affect results. Refer to the specific cooker manual and verify doneness, especially for meat and poultry.
